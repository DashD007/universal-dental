# Home (/)

## Scope

- **Route:** `/`, the landing page.
- **Visitor mode:** Persuade.
- **Audience:**
  - Dentists and clinics: a chair is down, or a refurbishment is planned.
  - Hospitals and institutions: comparing service and supply partners.
- **Actions:**
  - Primary: Book a service, which goes to the `#quote` form.
  - Secondary: Call the workshop.
- **Proof allowed:** confirmed facts only.
  - Multi-brand chair expertise.
  - Genuine or OEM-equivalent parts, with a written warranty on repairs.
  - Service and supply from one partner, including installation.
  - Certified technicians.
  - No numbers, ratings, response-time promises or invented clients.
  - Testimonials and before/after photos are marked placeholders until real ones arrive.
- **Structure:** pinned by the user to the Liftline reference sections. The sections are:
  1. Hero with a background image and two buttons
  2. Chair-systems ticker
  3. Services
  4. Before/after refurbishment slider
  5. Service visit steps
  6. Ortho-medical equipment
  7. Maintenance plans, with no prices
  8. Why Universal Dental
  9. Testimonials (placeholders)
  10. FAQ
  11. Quote form

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Home proves that Universal Dental knows the chair from the inside: every system, across manufacturers, repaired with genuine parts and covered in writing. It also shows that the same team supplies the equipment around the chair. It refuses the category default of a blue-white clinic, a smiling stock dentist, and three icon cards under a centred headline.

**OWN-WORLD:** the established world from `app/theme.css` and `/styleguide`.
- **Grounds:** bone and sand.
- **Bands:** moss fields at page scale, used as full-width bands, not accents.
- **Actions:** clay, on pill-shaped controls.
- **Type:** Fraunces for display (including the chair-systems ticker), Geist for body text, Geist Mono only for part numbers and specs.
- **Shapes:** soft card radii and hairline rules.
- **Photography:** warm, close to the work, with equipment, copper and upholstery tones.
- **Headings:** no small labels above headings.

**STORY:** The visitor sees a real chair and the promise. They learn which systems are covered and how a visit works. They see a refurbishment for themselves, learn the same partner supplies equipment, and compare the care plans. They ask a question, then send the chair's make, model and fault.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:**
- **Image:** a full-bleed clinic chair photo, nearly as tall as the screen, under a moss gradient that is heaviest bottom-left.
- **Copy:** a large Fraunces headline bottom-left, a two-line subhead, then the clay "Book a service" button with a light outline "Call" button beside it.
- **Proof strip:** along the base of the hero, three confirmed proof points separated by hairlines.

**FORM:**
- **Structure:** the Liftline section order, pinned by the user, so no roll was run.
- **Signature interaction:** a drag or keyboard before/after comparison of a refurbished chair.
- **Motion grammar:** the hero photo settles on load, and the systems ticker drifts. Both are disabled under reduced motion.
- **Seed:** seed:8060b9bd052213ef. The roll was recorded; the section structure stays user-pinned to the Liftline reference.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
